---
title: Good checkpoints
when: when you're about to write a checkpoint
tools: [checkpoint]
---
A checkpoint is your own note to the next session of you. It is all you'll have of this one.

- **thread**: the piece of work, in a few words, the same each time you return to it.
- **doing / done / next**: concrete; a file, a command, a name. "Write knowledge/index.md", not "continue".
- **learned**: what you'd want to know next time, including what didn't work and why.
- **blockers**: what you're waiting on, and from whom.
- Keep it short. Long checkpoints bury the one line that matters.
