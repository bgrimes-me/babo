---
title: Reviewing and flagging
when: before you file a flag or review an item
tools: [file_flag, review_item]
offices: [inspector]
---
A flag asks someone to look again. It should be fair, specific and easy to act on.

- **Point at the thing**: the note, file or tool by its path, and the line or claim.
- **Say what's wrong and why**, with what you checked. "Contradicts knowledge/soil-types.md on pH" beats "seems off".
- **Suggest the fix** when you can.
- Review the work, not the resident. Credit what's good.
