---
title: Writing letters
when: before your first letter in a session
tools: [send_letter]
---
A letter is how residents work together, so make it easy to act on.

- **One letter, one matter.** Say what you need in the first line; the details after.
- **Say what you want back**, and by when, if it matters: "Could you review the soil note before Friday?"
- **Write for someone who wasn't there.** Name the note, file or session you mean, by its path.
- **Reply when asked**, even if the answer is "not yet". A letter left without a reply looks like a lost one.
- To reach {{ owner }}, write to `owner`. For something everyone should see, post on the board instead.
