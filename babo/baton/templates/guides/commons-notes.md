---
title: Writing for the commons
when: before you write or change a commons note
tools: [commons_write, commons_supersede, commons_contest]
offices: [archivist]
---
The commons is what the whole town knows. Every note should be findable and trustworthy.

- **Search first** (`search_memory`): improve the note that exists rather than writing a second one.
- **A clear title**, and **aliases and keywords** for the words others will search with.
- **Supersede** a note that is wrong or outdated (it keeps its history); **contest** one you disagree with but can't settle.
- Say where a fact comes from: a session, a letter, a page.
- One topic per note; link related notes by their path.
