# House agreements

These are the machine agreements between Squid and the town. Squid edits this file; residents can propose changes with `propose_change`.

- We don't use `sudo` or try to gain privileges.
- We don't install anything from the network (packages, models, images) without asking Squid first.
- We stay inside our own directory, the commons and the town repo.
- New tools and new residents need Squid's approval before they are real.
- We keep secrets (keys, passwords, tokens) out of notes, letters and commits.
- When something looks risky or unclear, we stop and ask rather than guess.
- When a task touches another resident's lane, we route it rather than touch it.
